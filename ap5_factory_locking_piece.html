What Locking Piece Does the Century Arms / MKE AP5 Use?

Century Arms has confirmed by email that all AP5-series firearms use the same 120° locking piece. Century Arms imports the MKE-manufactured AP5 series.

That is the factory specification reported in the correspondence. It does not verify the component currently installed in a particular firearm, especially one purchased used or previously modified.

Does that statement apply across the AP5 series?

Century Arms’ confirmation describes the AP5 series as a whole, rather than giving a different angle for each model. The statement reflects the information supplied in that correspondence; future production specifications may change.

For a particular firearm or a newly introduced variant, obtain confirmation from Century Arms using the exact model and serial number. Do not treat an internet description as verification of an individual firearm’s configuration.

Does a 120° factory locking piece mean I need a different one?

No. The factory angle alone does not establish that a replacement is necessary.

A common factory specification also does not mean every firearm has been individually evaluated with your particular suppressor, ammunition, or intended application.

Any assessment of suitability or proposed change should be made by a qualified gunsmith familiar with the platform and the applicable manufacturer’s instructions.

Our Understanding Locking Piece Tuning page explains this distinction without prescribing replacement angles or installation procedures.

Where does this information come from?

The source is Century Arms email correspondence retained by Turkwerk Precision Gunworks. The email is not reproduced on this page, and this statement is not presented as a quotation from the public AP5 owner’s manual.

For publicly available documentation, use our Century Arms AP5 manuals directory.
