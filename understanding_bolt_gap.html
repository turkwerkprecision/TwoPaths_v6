Understanding Bolt Gap

What it means for your new host.

What bolt gap means

Bolt gap is the small clearance between the bolt head and bolt carrier when the firearm is prepared in the specified inspection condition. It is one indicator of the relationship between components in the roller-delayed operating system.

It matters because a firearm can appear normal externally while an internal measurement falls outside its manufacturer’s limits. Conversely, an acceptable bolt-gap measurement does not, by itself, prove that the entire firearm is correctly assembled or reliable.

Why the measurement can change

Bolt gap is not necessarily constant throughout a firearm’s service life. Component settling and wear can change the relationship between the interacting surfaces. Changes to components such as the bolt head or locking piece can also affect the measurement.

A previous reading therefore does not establish the condition after a parts change. Record the firearm’s configuration alongside each measurement so comparisons remain meaningful. An unexpected change requires evaluation rather than an assumption that it is normal.

Can an out-of-spec gap be corrected?

In some circumstances, a qualified gunsmith can bring bolt gap within specification using appropriately selected rollers. Whether that is an appropriate correction depends on the underlying condition.

An acceptable final number alone does not establish that the cause has been identified or resolved. This page does not provide roller-selection or replacement instructions.

For a newly purchased firearm, our recommendation is to start with a host that already meets its manufacturer’s specification. A known out-of-spec condition should be resolved with the retailer or manufacturer before it becomes a customer-funded troubleshooting project.

When an in-store inspection is unavailable

A retailer may prohibit the inspection or require a trigger lock to remain engaged. That leaves the measurement unknown; it does not establish that the firearm passes.

If the first permitted inspection identifies an out-of-spec condition, preserve the firearm’s original configuration and promptly engage the manufacturer or importer’s warranty support before making changes.

Professional validation without becoming your own technician

Purchase the firearm through your preferred dealer. Then put the host through Turkwerk Initial Host Validation.

The service is intended to document the starting condition, evaluate the host against defined acceptance criteria, and complete the agreed testing without requiring you to diagnose and modify it yourself.

A host that successfully completes validation is returned meeting the applicable acceptance criteria, with its tested configuration, results, and limitations documented in a reliability report. If it cannot meet those criteria, the outcome is a documented hold, manufacturer referral, or decline, rather than a passing report.

Validation establishes what was inspected and demonstrated under the recorded conditions. It does not guarantee malfunction-free operation under every future configuration or use.
