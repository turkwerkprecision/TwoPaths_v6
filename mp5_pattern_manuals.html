Finding bolt-gap information

An owner’s manual and an armorer’s manual serve different purposes. Do not assume that an owner’s manual contains every inspection procedure or acceptance limit.

When reviewing bolt-gap information, establish:

  • Whether it applies to your exact manufacturer and model.
  • The condition in which the measurement must be taken.
  • Whether the stated limits concern a new firearm, a firearm after break-in, or a firearm already in service.

If the applicable procedure or specification is missing or unclear, obtain written clarification from the manufacturer or importer. Do not substitute another brand’s specification.
